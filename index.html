Survive

  • Your ship fires automatically. Only the white core can be hit.
  • Dash through danger — you're untouchable mid-dash.
  • Graze bullets and chain kills to charge your Ultimate. Your pilot class replaces Overdrive: Striker erases every bullet and doubles fire and damage, Engineer raises a bullet-eating dome with extra wingmen, Ghost slows time around you and turns grazes into strikes. All of them double your score.
  • Pick your class and two passives on the Pilot screen of the campaign map. Pilot Rank unlocks more.
  • Collect data shards to level up and draft upgrades. Build around up to 4 weapon modules.
  • Clear sectors for bonus picks. Every third sector hides a boss.

Campaign

  • Pick a star system, then jump along a route: combat, elite, market, dock, anomaly, then the boss.
  • You earn credits in a run. Extract after the boss to bank them all; die and keep half. Spend them in the Hangar on ships, gear and paint.
  • Systems 2-4 open with a Supply Drop: free upgrade picks before the first jump.
  • Anomalies are risk-for-reward choices. Black Market and Docks heal and upgrade mid-run.
