# GENESIS campaign card

Opaque 2:1 environmental illustration for the main campaign progress map. Built-in imagegen uses the accepted gate and far skyline as references. Preserve the system identity, dark industrial materials and fine pixel detail. Quiet lower area supports runtime labels; text, frames and status are rendered separately.

Source: `sources/campaign-card-v1.png`; runtime: `src/assets/backgrounds/genesis/campaign-card-v1.png` (768×384 RGBA, nearest-neighbor export). Exact prompt: `campaign-card-prompt-v1.txt`.

CSS supplies grayscale discovered, heavily obscured undiscovered, and full-color cleared states. The source is opaque and needs no alpha-edge checks.
