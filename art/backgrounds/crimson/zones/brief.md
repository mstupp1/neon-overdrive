# Crimson zone layers

Spore Fields and Hive Trench each replace only the near skyline and near deck. Keep the ringed planet, far industrial skyline and far deck to anchor Crimson's identity. Baseline: references/baseline-top.png captured from isolated live preview on 2026-10-18.

Spore Fields: asymmetric fungal caps, hanging spore sacs and infected refinery roots. Hive Trench: ribbed chitin buttresses, burrow apertures and encased pipes. Near-black burgundy/navy material with sparse dull rose/crimson edge light. Scenery must recede behind shots and the reactive grid.

Skyline: transparent 720×480 export, bottom-aligned broad shallow silhouette, most of upper frame clear. Deck: transparent 360×360 export, overhead isolated clusters in side lanes, clear middle aisle, generous clear top/bottom margins. Used in top, side and pursuit cameras. No baked sky, floor, grid, fog, gameplay lights, UI, text, ships or collision cues.

Built-in imagegen sources and exact prompts are recorded in manifest.json; original exports remain unchanged.
