# OMEGA

Cyan radial iris core. Selected top-v1, side-v1 and chase-v1. The side is a flattened saucer; the front is nearly circular because the subject is radial. The measured iris is registered to world center in each export. Top core rotates; generated side/front stay level to preserve perspective while rings, radial nodes, shards and data tethers continue moving. Reviewed all three views, cached magenta phase-three core/node variants, flash and light/dark alpha edges.

Created with built-in imagegen. Full originals: `sources/`; exact prompts: `prompts/`; selected registration: `selection.json`; source/prompt/reference/output hashes and uniform export transforms: `manifest.json`. Runtime images: `src/assets/bosses/omega/`.

Validation on 2026-10-16: body export completed; the combined rendering suite passed 46 tests. Browser review used the isolated-save boss preview, with all 12 boss/camera combinations loading, phase controls and keyboard-operable selectors, light/dark backgrounds, normal/pixel look, and a 390×844 responsive check. A stale cache on the old port and an iframe postMessage origin error were resolved before the final pass. No new browser warnings/errors appeared in the corrected preview. This was a visual and encounter smoke check, not a complete campaign playthrough or verification of every attack permutation.
