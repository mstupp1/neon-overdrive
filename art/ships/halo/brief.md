# HALO generated hull set

Three built-in imagegen views are retained in `sources/`, with the exact selected prompts under `prompts/`. The top master preserves HALO's compact carrier fuselage, short winglets, stock rose-pink identity, multi-door bay, paired spark emitters, twin engines and broad circular structural drone-rail. The four docking details are integrated into the rail; there are no detached drones in the static art. The side master faces right and foreshortens the ring into an attached rim. The pursuit view looks astern, with the rail compressed into a wide arc and both open engine apertures facing the viewer.

Three 256x256 transparent RGBA runtime files were exported with `tools/ship_assets.py`. The sources, exact prompts, reference provenance, fullsource dimensions, measured anchors, camera and export notes, and file hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`; normalized fullsource anchor coordinates are in `src/render/shipImageSpecs/halo.js`.

Visual review confirmed the carrier ring, emitters, open engines, stock palette and transparent alpha. Browser firing and exhaust alignment remain for the parent integration pass.
