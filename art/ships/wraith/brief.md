# WRAITH generated ship views

WRAITH is a fast duelist with a narrow central body, two parallel side booms, twin lance apertures and two aft engines. The generated top and side views retain the mint stock accent. The first pursuit result was replaced after review with a low rear camera showing two large empty engine faces, short foreshortened body and low wide wing projection. Anchors are measured in original source pixels.

Runtime exports are 256×256 RGBA PNGs generated with `tools/ship_assets.py`; the manifest records source and runtime hashes, prompt paths and measured original-source anchor pixels.
